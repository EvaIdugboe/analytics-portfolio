# Eva Idugboe | Analytics Portfolio

A selection of data analysis case studies examining customer behaviour, digital marketing performance and commercial outcomes. The focus is on how evidence can inform business decisions, with the assumptions and limitations stated clearly.

## Featured projects

### [AfriTech Electronics | Revenue, Customer and Product Risk Analysis](AfriTech_Electronics/README.md)
**PostgreSQL · SQL · Power BI · DAX**

A case study investigating AfriTech's revenue decline, customer revenue concentration, product recall exposure and social media performance. Includes selected aggregated charts, read-only SQL examples and methodology notes.

### [BrightCart | Retail Traffic and Conversion Analysis](BrightCart_Retail/README.md)
**Tableau · Google Sheets**

A retail analysis of traffic sources, timing, mobile behaviour and conversion patterns, based on the original project presentation.

## Approach

The work combines business-question framing, data exploration, metric checks, dashboard analysis and recommendations. Proposed actions are not presented as business outcomes already achieved.

## Public sharing

These are portfolio case studies using supplied project material. Raw datasets, customer-level records and the source Power BI model are deliberately excluded pending confirmation of public-sharing rights. All reported commercial metrics relate to the case-study data.
